Lotus 1 Neo: the first Lotus on the Neo Geo (fan project, in development)

After Lotus Neo (Lotus III), here comes the first episode: Lotus Esprit Turbo Challenge (Magnetic Fields / Gremlin, 1990), ported to the Neo Geo with the same engine, in C and 68000 assembly. The tracks, race rules, texts and music come from the original Amiga game; the graphics are those of the AGA edition (256 colours).

Already working

  • All 32 tracks of the game plus the practice track, with their original curves, hills, colours and number of laps.
  • Championship on 3 levels (7, 10 and 15 races): 20 cars, reversed grid, the original points and qualification in the top 10; the opponents get faster from track to track, as on the Amiga.
  • Fuel and pit stops, with the garage screen of the AGA edition and its animated mechanics.
  • Full screen for 1 player, split screen for 2, like the AGA edition.
  • The Esprit Turbo SE, with automatic or manual gears, and the Lotus 1 dashboard (gear, speed, revs, fuel, position, laps).
  • Panoramas, roadside objects, cars and gantries from the AGA edition.
  • The whole original presentation: logos, the Esprit switching on its headlights, spec sheets with the spinning Esprit, high scores for the 3 levels, track card before each race, results, standings, end of season with licence number, and name entry.
  • Original soundtrack: the game's 5 tunes, the car radio to pick the tune in the race (with the badly tuned radio noise on position 00), the sound effects and the engine.
  • High scores saved on MVS (separate from Lotus Neo's).

Controls

D-pad left / right = steer, A = accelerate (or up, set in Game Options), B or down = brake, C / D = gear up / down, START = pause.

Still to do

  • Full testing on MiSTer (display, sound, a whole game); the game already runs in MAME, with the MVS BIOS and UniBIOS.
  • Lotus 1's own handling and road width, a few road details.
  • The build tool, as for my other ports.
