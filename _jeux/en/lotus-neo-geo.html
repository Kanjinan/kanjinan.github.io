Lotus Neo: Lotus III on the Neo Geo, with its championship and endless tracks.

A fan-made Neo Geo port of the Amiga classic Lotus III: The Ultimate Challenge (Magnetic Fields / Gremlin, 1992). The game was rebuilt from scratch in C and 68000 assembly, from the original game's data and code: the physics, the rivals' AI and the track generator are those of Lotus III.

What's in it

  • Pseudo-3D road drawn line by line (raster effect), with real bends, hills and a horizon that follows the road: a steady 30 fps in races (the original ran at 25), 20 fps with two players.
  • 3 cars: Esprit Turbo SE, Elan SE and M200, with automatic or 5-speed manual gears.
  • Championship over 3 levels (7, 10 and 15 races) with the original points, qualification, fuel and pit stops, or single races against the clock.
  • RECS: the original track generator runs as is on the Neo Geo's 68000. Enter a code or set the sliders to create a new track, and keep up to 9 codes in memory (DEFINE).
  • 13 sceneries: forest, night, fog, snow, desert, motorway, marsh, storm, roadworks, rally, wind, Futureworld and mountain, with tunnels, turbo zones, jumps, fords, motorway traffic, lightning, snow and rain.
  • 2 players in split screen, in single races as well as in the championship.
  • 19 rivals, with the original parody driver names.
  • Original intro, menus and screens, demo mode, high scores saved on MVS.
  • Original soundtrack: the ProTracker modules converted for the Neo Geo, the car radio to pick the music, and all the sound effects.

Controls

D-pad = steer, A = accelerate, B = brake, C / D = shift up / down, START = pause. In menus, A or START confirms, B goes back.

Installation

The game is not distributed as ROMs: a tool builds it from your Lotus III Amiga disks (see below). It makes the files for NeoSD / MiSTer, MAME, Darksoft and BackBit Platinum MVS. Runs on MAME, MiSTer and NeoSD.
