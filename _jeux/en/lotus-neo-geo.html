LOTUS NEO — a Lotus III racing engine for the Neo Geo

A fan-made Neo Geo port of the Amiga classic Lotus III: The Ultimate Challenge. The game was rebuilt from scratch in C and 68000 assembly, using the original game's data.

Features

  • Fast pseudo-3D road with a per-scanline raster effect, at a smooth 60 fps
  • The 7 races of the Level 1 championship, each with its own scenery: Futureworld, Rally, Motorway, Wind, Mountain, Forest, Storm
  • 3 cars to pick from: Esprit Turbo SE, Elan SE, M200
  • Championship mode with the original points system and qualification, or single races
  • 6 AI rivals who overtake, block and bump, using the original parody driver names
  • Original title screens, car and track select, panoramic horizons and roadside scenery
  • Original ProTracker soundtrack, plus engine, crash, skid and overtaking sound effects

Controls: A = accelerate, B = brake, D-pad = steer. A or START confirms in menus, B goes back.

Runs on MAME, MiSTer and NeoSD (.neo file).

Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with the rights holders of Lotus III.
