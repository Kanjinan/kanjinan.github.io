Undercover Cops / Neo Geo port (work in progress)

An unofficial port of Undercover Cops (Irem, 1992), the M92 arcade beat 'em up, to the Neo Geo (MVS/AES), written in C with ngdevkit.

The goal is accuracy. Backgrounds, animations, enemy behaviour, damage values and spawn scripts aren't recreated by eye. They are taken from the original game by reading its V33 code and tracing it frame by frame in MAME, then converted for the Neo Geo hardware.

Already playable

  • All 5 stages, from stage 1 to Dr. CRAYBORN's airship, with their bosses: FRANSOWORS, MOGURALTAN, BALBAROTCH and Dr. CRAYBORN
  • All three characters, Zan, Matt and Rosa, with combos, grabs, throws, special moves and running attacks
  • The game's carryable objects: steel pillar, burning barrels, DANGER crates, motorbike, bundle of pipes, newspapers, burning debris
  • Pickups and their effects (health, points, stars), end-of-stage tallies and the FINAL ASSESSMENT
  • Good and bad endings, continue, game over, high-score table and name entry
  • The full attract mode: title screen, character profiles, demos
  • The original music and sound effects, played back on the Neo Geo's sound hardware
  • Background effects: pulsing traffic lights, the boss's red carpet, ceiling hatches, collapsing walkways

In progress

  • Polishing pass, stage by stage, checked against the original game
  • A few details still to capture: Rosa's special-move lightning, the boss health bar, a handful of values that are still estimates

Notes

  • Fan project, not affiliated with Irem or the rights holders of Undercover Cops.
  • Development build: expect bugs and differences from the original. Feedback is welcome in the comments.
