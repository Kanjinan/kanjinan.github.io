U.N. Squadron Neo: Capcom's shoot 'em up on the Neo Geo (fan project, in development)

A fan port of the arcade game U.N. Squadron (Area 88 in Japan), Capcom's side-scrolling shooter (1989, CPS1), to the Neo Geo (MVS/AES). Fly mercenary missions as Shin Kazama, Mickey Simon or Greg Gates, earn money and spend it on new jets and weapons between missions.

How it works

It is not a remake or a re-creation: the original 68000 program runs as-is on the Neo Geo. The CPS1 board and the Neo Geo both use a Motorola 68000, so the game logic, enemy patterns, timing and difficulty stay those of the arcade. Only the hardware accesses change: each instruction that touches the CPS1 video chips, inputs or sound is redirected to a translation layer that rebuilds the picture on Neo Geo hardware:

  • CPS1 sprites become Neo Geo sprites;
  • the scrolling background layers are rebuilt as sprite strips;
  • the text and HUD go onto the fix layer;
  • palettes are converted on the fly during vertical blank.

This is the same technique as my Ghouls'n Ghosts port.

Planned

  • The complete arcade game, all missions and bosses, at 60 fps
  • The original music and sound effects, recorded from the arcade game and played back as ADPCM by the YM2610
  • The 384-pixel CPS1 screen reframed to 320 pixels, with a rearranged HUD
  • MVS settings in the BIOS menu: difficulty, damage, continues
  • AES mode: credits on START and pause
  • An options screen at boot: difficulty, damage, buttons, autofire, sound test
  • Testing on real hardware and on MiSTer

Current status

  • ROM set analysis done: CPS-B-11 board, AR24B graphics mapper, OKI + YM2151 sound.
  • Next steps: booting the original program on the Neo Geo, then graphics, playable missions, sound and options.

Notes
