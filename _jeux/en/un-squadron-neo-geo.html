U.N. Squadron Neo: Capcom's shoot 'em up on the Neo Geo (fan project, in development)

A fan port of the arcade game U.N. Squadron (Area 88 in Japan), Capcom's side-scrolling shooter (1989, CPS1), to the Neo Geo (MVS/AES). Fly mercenary missions as Shin Kazama, Mickey Simon or Greg Gates, earn money and spend it on new jets and weapons between missions.

How it works

It is not a remake or a re-creation: the original 68000 program runs as-is on the Neo Geo. The CPS1 board and the Neo Geo both use a Motorola 68000, so the game logic, enemy patterns, timing and difficulty stay those of the arcade. Only the hardware accesses change: each instruction that touches the CPS1 video chips, inputs or sound is redirected to a translation layer that rebuilds the picture on Neo Geo hardware:

  • CPS1 sprites become Neo Geo sprites;
  • the scrolling background layers are rebuilt as sprite strips;
  • the text and HUD go onto the fix layer;
  • palettes are converted on the fly during vertical blank.

This is the same technique as my Ghouls'n Ghosts port.

Already working

  • The original program runs on the Neo Geo: full attract mode (title, pilot and plane presentation), pilot select, briefing, shop and missions
  • The first mission is playable from start to finish at full speed (60 fps), for one or two simultaneous players
  • All ten missions start and run without crashing (automated runs); the very busy mission 2 boss has been optimised to stay at 60 fps
  • The original music, sound effects and voices, recorded from the arcade game and played back in ADPCM by the YM2610
  • Scenery that passes in front of the planes (the cranes of mission 1, the jungle trees…) handled mission by mission, as on the CPS1
  • The 384-pixel screen reframed to 320 pixels: HUD laid out again (one and two players) and kept inside the visible area of CRT TVs, title logo redrawn, pilot select, briefing and shop rescaled
  • An options screen at boot: difficulty (8 levels), damage, continue, free play, autofire; with no input for 30 seconds the game starts on its own (MVS cabinets)
  • AES mode: free play by default, START begins the game
  • First MiSTer feedback taken into account: palettes are now written during vertical blank, with no more glitches during fades
