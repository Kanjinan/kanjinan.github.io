Metal Slug Enhanced: the original Metal Slug, set up the way you like.

A ROM hack of the first Metal Slug (Nazca / SNK, 1996) for the Neo Geo. The game stays the same: the hack adds an options menu, built directly into the original 68000 code. With the default settings, the game is identical to the original.

The START / OPTION screen

When a game starts, a START / OPTION screen appears, on arcade (MVS) as on console (AES). The word ENHANCED is shown under the logo, on the title screen and in attract mode.

The OPTION menu

  • LEVEL: difficulty (EASY, NORMAL, HARD, MVS).
  • MISSION: starting mission, 1 to 6.
  • HERO: number of lives, 1 to 9, or infinite lives.
  • LANGUAGE: tutorial language: automatic, English, Portuguese, Japanese, and Spanish, a language hidden in the original game.
  • ARMS: starting weapon for every life: handgun, Heavy Machine Gun, Rocket Launcher, Flame Shot or Shotgun.
  • KEEP ARMS: when you die, you keep your weapon and its ammo.
  • AMMO: infinite ammo.
  • BOMBS: starting grenades (10, 20, 30, 50, 99) or infinite grenades.
  • TIME: the timer (game setting, 60 to 90 seconds, or infinite).
  • EFFECTS: FULL or LIGHT. LIGHT removes a few purely decorative visual effects to reduce slowdown. In mission 5, the busiest one, the share of slowed-down frames drops from 25% to 7%.

Up and Down pick a line, Left and Right change the value, A on EXIT confirms. On MVS (and in MAME), settings are saved. On AES, they are kept until power-off. In two-player games, each player gets their own weapon and ammo.

Installation

The hack comes as IPS patches, bundled in a tool that builds the game from your MAME romset mslug.zip (see below). Runs on MAME, MiSTer and NeoSD. The tool also builds the files for Darksoft and BackBit Platinum MVS. The IPS patches are included in the archive too, if you prefer to apply them with your own tool.
