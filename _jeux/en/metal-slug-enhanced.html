Metal Slug Enhanced: the original Metal Slug, made better (project in preparation)

A ROM hack of the first Metal Slug (Nazca / SNK, 1996) for the Neo Geo. The game stays the same, with a series of small improvements made directly to the original 68000 code.

How it works

Each improvement is a separate patch, applied to the game's program after checking the original bytes. The result is released only as an IPS patch, to apply to your own mslug ROM: no data from the original game is included.

Already in

  • 30 grenades at the start and on every respawn, instead of 10.
