# About

I make homebrews and fan ports for the **Neo Geo** (MVS/AES, NeoSD, MiSTer, Darksoft, BackBit) and the **SEGA Mega Drive / Genesis**.

My games can be downloaded from my [itch.io]({{ site.itch }}) page. Feedback and bug reports are welcome in each game's comments.

The ports are non-commercial fan projects, not affiliated with the rights holders of the original games.
