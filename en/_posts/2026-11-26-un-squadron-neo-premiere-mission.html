First news from U.N. Squadron Neo, my fan port of Capcom's CPS1 shoot 'em up to the Neo Geo, announced yesterday. The original 68000 program already boots and plays on the Neo Geo.

What's new

  • Full attract mode, pilot select, briefing, shop: the whole arcade flow
  • The first mission playable from start to finish at 60 fps, for one or two simultaneous players
  • All ten missions start and run without crashing in automated runs; the mission 2 boss, with its dozens of animated objects, has been optimised so it no longer slows down
  • Original music, sound effects and voices, recorded from the arcade game and played back in ADPCM by the YM2610
  • Scenery in front of the planes (cranes, jungle trees…) handled mission by mission, as on the CPS1
  • Screen reframed from 384 to 320 pixels: HUD laid out again and kept inside the visible area of CRT TVs, logo redrawn, briefing and shop rescaled
  • Options screen at boot (difficulty, damage, continue, free play, autofire) and AES mode
  • First MiSTer feedback: no more glitches during fades

Next steps: checking missions 2 to 10 to the end with their bosses, testing on real hardware, then the ROM builder. Questions or feedback? Let me know in the comments!
