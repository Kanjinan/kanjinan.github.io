As with Ghouls'n Ghosts, you can now build U.N. Squadron Neo yourself: builder 0.1 is available on the game page. It contains no data from the original game: it rebuilds the ROMs from your own MAME romset (unsquad.zip, USA version) and records the original game's sound in MAME. You need Windows, Python 3.9+ and MAME; it takes a few minutes. You get the files for NeoSD / MiSTer, Darksoft, BackBit and MAME.

New since this morning

  • A French translation, selected in the options screen (LANGUAGE): story, pilot profiles, briefings, shop and dialogue, with accented letters
  • Starting mission (1 to 10) in the options screen, replacing autofire
  • Pause on AES with START
  • The NK logo at boot, as in Ghouls'n Ghosts
  • MiSTer fixes: no more black dots at the top of the screen or in the mission 1 sky, correct palettes during fades
  • Briefing and pilot select rescaled across the full width, no more tearing on the briefing character's face, title screen at the right speed

This is a test release: the first mission has been checked from start to finish, the later ones run but have not been played through yet. Feedback from real hardware and MiSTer is welcome. Questions or feedback? Let me know in the comments!
