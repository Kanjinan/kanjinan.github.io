An update to my Neo Geo port of Lotus III: Lotus Neo 1.1 is out. It brings the game even closer to the Amiga version and fixes the display on MiSTer.

Racing

  • Bridges on the roadworks and motorway scenery, as in the original: a full-width deck, pillars at the roadside and the rest of the track visible underneath.
  • Rain and snow redone after the original, over the whole height of the screen.
  • Water spray when driving through puddles, fords and marshes.
  • Lap races: the starting grid is before the line again, as in the original.
  • Speedometer moved back to its original place, marsh sky fixed, more rocks in the mountains.
  • In 2-player mode, the "… AHEAD" message when the other player is just in front.
  • When you pick a song, it replaces the engine and sound effects and starts before the countdown, as on the Amiga. With NO MUSIC, you keep the engine.

Menus and presentation

  • The sparkles on the Gremlin logo at startup.
  • Fades at the original speed throughout the game, menus included.
  • The car radio works like the original: left / right to pick the song (or NO MUSIC), the title in the middle of the CD player, the LCD with track and time, and equalizers that move with the music.
  • The title, demo and high-score cycle uses the original timings: the title music is no longer cut short, and you can start a game right away.
  • All in-game texts are in English.

Display

  • The road drawing is tuned for MiSTer: no more black glitches on the left of the screen or in the sky, and the start gantry no longer shakes.
  • In 2-player mode, player 2 entering a tunnel no longer scrambles their view.

As before, download the tool from the game's page, put your 2 Lotus III Amiga disks and the MAME neogeo.zip BIOS next to it, then double-click Construire - Build.bat.

Enjoy the ride! 🏁
