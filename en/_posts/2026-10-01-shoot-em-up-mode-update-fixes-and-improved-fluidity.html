Fixed Bugs :

Black screens and crashes between stages: the game could freeze on a black screen or display an error message when transitioning between stages, particularly late in the game (stages 2-3 to 2-5) or after using multiple Continues. The cause has been identified and fixed; you should now be able to play through all stages in one go.

Incomplete HUD at the start of stage 2-1: the top-left corner of the HUD was not displaying. It is now back.

White flashes before the first cutscene: small white artifacts would appear briefly just before the image of the plane. These have been removed.

Stage 2-4B: background elements would slide across the top of the screen before the stage began. Fixed; the ship now enters from the bottom, as it should in a vertical stage.

⚡ Improvements

Smoother scrolling in stages 2-3, 2-4, 2-4B, and 2-5: parallax now updates every frame.
Reduced system load: the game is less demanding on the console, which limits slowdowns when there are many enemies on screen.
Faster transitions: the black screen before the bonus zone is about 3 seconds shorter, and the pause before cutscenes is 1 second shorter.

Thank you for your feedback—it helps me immensely! Please don't hesitate to report any bugs you encounter.
