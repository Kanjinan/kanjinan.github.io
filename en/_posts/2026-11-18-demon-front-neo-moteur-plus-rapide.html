An update on Demon Front Neo, my fan port of IGS's run and gun to the Neo Geo. Today was mostly about making the engine faster and more faithful to the original.

What's new

  • Faster engine: optimised game loop (object drawing rewritten in assembly), up to 20% faster in the busiest stages
  • More enemies and scenery shown when the screen is busy; the hero no longer vanishes behind explosions
  • No more black areas in the STATION stage: sprite flicker now drops small items first, not the scenery
  • Attract mode demos compared turn by turn with the original: player entry, order of actions, camera and enemy rules fixed
  • No more sections stuck when everything is destroyed: all six stages have been checked through to the end
  • Test options in OPTIONS: starting stage and section, invincibility

Still to come: two-player mode (player 2 HUD and testing), difficulty in OPTIONS and the last scenes that slow down. Questions or feedback? Let me know in the comments!
