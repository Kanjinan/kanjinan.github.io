An update to my cartridge port of the Neo Geo CD Samurai Shodown RPG: Samurai Spirits RPG 1.1 is out. Thanks to the tester who reported the first two bugs!

What's new

  • The 2nd story's music finally plays. The game waited for a sound file that only the CD loaded, and skipped sending the music along with it. This bug hit every platform, MiSTer included.
  • Shops no longer freeze. The translated shopkeeper lines, longer than the Japanese ones, maxed out the CPU, and the item list was read from the wrong place. You can buy, sell and leave again.
  • 544 shopkeeper lines shortened to fit their box, in French, English and Spanish.
  • Wider Buy / Sell / Exit menu: the cursor no longer overlaps the words. Shop names fit in their 6 letters (Inn, Remedy, Gifts…).
  • Cleaner shop list: no more small stray icons before the prices (Japanese benefits too), and no more 個 kanji after the quantity outside Japanese.

What about emulators?

The ROM targets MiSTer, Neo SD and real Neo Geo hardware. Geolith (RetroArch) and MAME only read the first 256 KB of the sound CPU ROM, while it is 512 KB: music stays silent in several areas there, including the 2nd story. It cannot be fixed in the ROM, as there is no room left.

How to get it

As before, download the 1.1 tool from the game page, put your Japanese CD image next to it (Redump dump, .cue and its 32 .bin files) and double-click Construire - Build.bat. These fixes were checked in MAME: feedback from MiSTer players is welcome in the comments! ⚔️
