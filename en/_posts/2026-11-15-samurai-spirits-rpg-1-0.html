The Neo Geo CD's Samurai Shodown RPG is finally playable as a cartridge, and in English! Samurai Spirits RPG 1.0 rebuilds Shinsetsu Samurai Spirits: Bushidou Retsuden (1997) as a standard Neo Geo ROM for MiSTer, with no CD drive and no loading times.

What's in it

  • 4 languages in a single ROM: Japanese (original), French, English and Spanish, picked at power-on.
  • Full translation: story, dialogue, menus, items, techniques, shops, battles, save screen and GAME OVER.
  • A redesigned battle interface for Western languages, and a new font with accents.
  • Playable from start to finish with every character, memory card saves included.
  • Sound effects, battle cries, FM music and the 44 minutes of CD audio.

How to get it

Download the tool from the game's page, put your image of the Japanese CD next to it (Redump dump, the .cue and its 32 .bin files), then double-click Construire - Build.bat. In about a minute, you have the .neo file to copy to your MiSTer (Neo Geo core, 128 MB SDRAM module). The tool contains no data from the original game: it copies everything from your CD and only adds my own work.

Enjoy your journey through feudal Japan, and please report any bug or freeze in the comments, with the character and the place! ⚔️
