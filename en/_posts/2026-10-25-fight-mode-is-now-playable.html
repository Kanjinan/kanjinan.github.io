🇬🇧 Devlog — FIGHT MODE is now playable!

A new build of Warrior Man for Neo Geo is out, for NeoSD, BackBit Platinum MVS and Darksoft.

🥊 FIGHT MODE

  • FIGHT MODE is now playable in the public build. Marius sets out to find Lena and meets Lisa at the temple. Together they fight through the street, the bar, the tower, the harbor and the dojo.
  • Story scenes between stages, in 4 languages.
  • Lots of new opponents that change from stage to stage, plus a new boss for stage 1.
  • Smarter AI. Enemies take turns, surround you and sometimes dodge. They also have a heavy attack to watch out for. When there are three of them, they keep their distance so close combat doesn't get overwhelming.
  • Breakable objects: crates, barrels, trash cans and oil drums, some on fire or explosive. They can hide healing cans or bonus points. The oil drum is now smaller, it was too big on screen.
  • Special powers with electric effects (C button), hit counter, blood and explosions.
  • End-of-stage results: time, max combo, damage, objects, rank and bonus. Extra life at 20,000 points, then at 50,000 and every 50,000 after that.
  • A new ending. After Marius and Lisa leave at sunset, their former opponents stroll down the pier, replay the fight in their own words… and then leave too.

Thanks for playing, and feel free to leave feedback in the comments! 🎮
