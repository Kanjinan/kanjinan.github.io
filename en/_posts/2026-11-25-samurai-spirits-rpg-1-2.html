Samurai Spirits RPG 1.2 is out. A tester reported that 1.1 did not boot on the Neo SD Pro: garbled UniBIOS boot screen, unreadable language menu, then nothing. Thanks to them! This version is mainly about making the game fit on that cart.

What's new

  • ROM reduced from 909 to 765 Mbit. Sprites are stored more efficiently: each duplicate tile is now stored only once, and the sprites go from 65 to 51 MB without losing a single frame. The game is now under the Neo SD Pro's estimated 768 Mbit per-game limit.
  • No more graphics garbage at the top of the UniBIOS boot screen. The UniBIOS draws the cart's first tiles there, meant for a logo on official games; they are now blank. This also showed on MiSTer.
  • CD track music converted at 9.6 kHz instead of 12.7 kHz, to save 4 MB: it sounds a bit more muffled. Sound effects and FM tunes are unchanged.
  • Title screen: NK V1.2 signature.

The game was checked in MAME (same frames as 1.1) and on MiSTer, including the water animations in the 2nd story. It has not been tested on a Neo SD Pro yet: if you own one, your feedback is very welcome.

How to get it

Download the 1.2 tool from the game page, put your Japanese CD image next to it (Redump dump, .cue and its 32 .bin files) and double-click Construire - Build.bat. The .neo file is now about 96 MB. ⚔️
