Shoot Mode Update!

The SHOOT mode of Warrior Man has grown a lot: it's now a full horizontal shoot'em up for the Neo Geo, inspired by R-Type and the arcade classics of the 90s. You pilot the Thunder DWS through five stages to wipe out the alien fleet.

Five stages, five atmospheres

  • 2-1 Jungle Outpost: two-layer parallax scrolling, enemies that circle the screen and an asteroid field right after the boss.
  • 2-2 Earth Orbit: a mid-boss with its escort, then a boss that zooms in from the depth of space.
  • 2-3 Descent Corridor: a vertical descent between deadly walls, then a switch to horizontal multi-layer scrolling and two bosses in a row.
  • 2-4 Battleship Raid: a space station guarded by turrets, then a drift into a nebula and a full tour around a giant battleship. The camera follows the hull, its lights blink, arrows warn you before each turn, and a boss is waiting at the end.
  • 2-5 Final Fortress: the last stronghold and the final boss.

Weapons

  • Main shot, plus a charged shot that goes through every enemy in its path. Each kill with the same shot raises a X1 / X2 / X3 combo multiplier, which you keep until you get hit.
  • R-Type-style Force modules (up to two): send them forward or call them back, fire backwards with them, and power them up through 4 shot levels by collecting bonuses (homing missiles included).
  • A nova bomb: it uses up one module, makes every enemy on screen flash, then blows them up in a chain reaction. It also clears all enemy bullets.
  • Pickups for ship speed (5 levels, shown in the HUD) and extra lives.

Presentation

  • An intro with the news anchor and a starfield ending on the Moon, then READY on a black screen, sliding shutters and an animated STAGE 2-X card with the stage name typed out.
  • A hyperspace jump at the end of each stage, a WARNING banner before each boss, smooth background transitions, and big explosions.

And also

  • A high score table with name entry, a continue screen, several difficulty levels, and 4 languages (EN / FR / ES / JP).
  • An arcade-style attract mode: when the title screen sits idle, the game plays demos of SHOOT, FIGHT and ROAD.

Next steps: more polish and balancing, especially the difficulty curve of stages 2-4 and 2-5.
