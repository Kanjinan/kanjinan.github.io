My Neo Geo port of Lotus III: The Ultimate Challenge is ready to play! Lotus Neo 1.0 brings the whole 1992 Amiga game: the physics, the rivals' AI and even the track generator are those of the original game.

What's in it

  • A pseudo-3D road drawn line by line, with bends, hills and a horizon, at a steady 30 fps (the original ran at 25).
  • The championship over 3 levels (7, 10 and 15 races), with qualification, fuel and pit stops.
  • The original RECS generator, running as is on the Neo Geo's 68000: one code, one track.
  • 13 sceneries, tunnels, jumps, motorway traffic, storms, snow and rain.
  • 2 players in split screen, 3 cars, automatic or manual gears.
  • The original intro, menus and soundtrack, with the car radio to pick the music.

How to get it

Download the tool from the game's page, put your 2 Lotus III Amiga disks (ADF files) and the MAME neogeo.zip BIOS next to it, then double-click Construire - Build.bat. In about a minute, you have the files for NeoSD / MiSTer, MAME, Darksoft and BackBit. The tool contains no data from the original game: the graphics, tracks and music are rebuilt on your computer from the disks.
