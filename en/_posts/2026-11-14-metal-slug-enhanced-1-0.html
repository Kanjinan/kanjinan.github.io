My ROM hack of the first Metal Slug is finished! Metal Slug Enhanced 1.0 adds a full options menu to the 1996 game and changes nothing else: with the default settings, it is the original game.

What's in it

  • A START / OPTION screen when each game starts, on arcade as on console, with "ENHANCED" under the logo.
  • A starting weapon of your choice (Heavy Machine Gun, Rocket Launcher, Flame Shot, Shotgun), with the KEEP ARMS option to keep your weapon and ammo after dying.
  • Adjustable ammo, grenades, lives and timer, all the way to infinite.
  • A starting mission of your choice, 1 to 6.
  • The tutorial in Spanish, a language hidden in the original game.
  • An EFFECTS LIGHT option that removes a few decorative effects: in mission 5, the share of slowed-down frames drops from 25% to 7%.
  • Settings are saved on MVS.

How to get it

Download the tool from the game's page, put your MAME romset mslug.zip next to it and double-click Construire - Build.bat. In a few seconds you get the files for NeoSD / MiSTer, MAME, Darksoft and BackBit. The tool contains no data from the original game, only the IPS patches.
