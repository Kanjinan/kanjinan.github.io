An update to my ROM hack of the first Metal Slug: Metal Slug Enhanced 1.1 is out.

What's new

  • New BLOOD option: blood on or off (YES / NO), whatever the console's region. By default, the game's own setting is kept.
  • BLOOD replaces the KEEP ARMS option, which has been removed.
  • Simpler menu: BOMBS offers 10, 20 or infinite grenades; TIME offers NORMAL, 60 seconds or an infinite timer.
  • Settings saved by version 1.0 are not carried over: the menu starts from the default values on first launch.

As before, download the tool from the game's page, put your MAME romset mslug.zip next to it and double-click Construire - Build.bat.

Have fun! 🎮
