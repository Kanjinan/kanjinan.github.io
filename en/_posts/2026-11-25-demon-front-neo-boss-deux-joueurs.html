An update on Demon Front Neo, my fan port of IGS's run and gun to the Neo Geo, a week after the last post.

What's new

  • Two-player mode: player 2 can join at HERO CHOOSE or mid-game, with a CONTINUE for each player
  • All nine bosses checked one by one against the original: attacks, entrances and detachable parts now match; the enemies' special AI (including Twin Horn) and their turnarounds have been ported
  • The VALLEY attract-mode demo now plays exactly like the original from start to finish
  • Name entry for the ranking, with a pixel-perfect original background
  • Smoothness: the game logic always runs at 60 Hz; the display drops to 30 fps when the screen gets too busy and goes back to 60 as soon as it can. New FPS line in OPTIONS (AUTO, 60 or 30)
  • Off-screen enemies are lighter to process, and the Frozen boss throws fewer bubbles when the load is high
  • Background scenery from the original added (lava before the rail, lanterns…), not animated yet
  • Cannon machine: cannons and rock shown together, two barrels instead of three so the shells stay visible, and a softer hit flash on large enemies
  • No more sprite flicker: background layers hidden behind the scenery are no longer drawn, which avoids the 96-sprites-per-line limit (stage 1 door and platforms on MiSTer)
  • Black background on the STATION rail fixed, and the HERO CHOOSE flash is as smooth as the original

Still to come: showing the updated ranking, difficulty in OPTIONS, background animation, and the Twin Horn boss, which still slows down. Questions or feedback? Let me know in the comments!
